Post-Thyroidectomy Hematoma

Tense swelling, stridor, respiratory distress.

Action Steps:
  1. Call for Senior Help & Airway Cart immediately.
  2. Cut skin sutures & open clips at bedside.
  3. Evacuate blood clots with fingers to relieve airway pressure.
  4. Transfer to OR for formal hemostasis under light anesthesia.

Thoracic Duct Chyle Leak

Milky drain output (>500mL/day = High Output).

Management:
  1. Low output (<500mL): Medium-chain triglyceride (MCT) diet / NPO.
  2. Apply pressure dressing & continuous negative suction.
  3. Octreotide / Somatostatin therapy.
  4. High output (>1000mL x 5d): OR re-exploration / Thoracic duct ligation.

Microvascular Flap Congestion

Dark purple, brisk pinprick capillary refill (<1 sec) = Venous Occlusion.

Action Plan:
  1. Check neck dressings / tracheostomy ties for constriction.
  2. Position head neutral (avoid neck torsion).
  3. Immediate operative re-exploration for microvascular revision.

Carotid Blowout Syndrome

Sentinel bleed from necrotic irradiated neck wound.

Emergency Steps:
  1. Direct manual compression with gauze over carotid.
  2. Establish 2 large-bore IVs & rapid fluid/blood resuscitation.
  3. Emergency Endovascular Stenting / Embolization vs Ligation.
